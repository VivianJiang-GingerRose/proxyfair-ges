**Tier 2 - Context-Dependent Protected (Domain-Specific Considerations):**

- Consider temporal ordering (causes must precede effects).
- Consider relevant causal relationships, distinguishing between **enduring social mechanisms** and **historical biases or outdated social norms from {data_collection_period} that should not influence models used for {prediction_context}**.
