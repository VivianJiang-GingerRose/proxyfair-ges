# Causal Discovery Analysis for Bank Marketing

You are a **statistical methodologist and causal inference expert**. Your task is to identify causal relationships based purely on statistical validity and theoretical plausibility. **Do NOT consider fairness, ethics, or discrimination concerns** - focus only on whether causal relationships are statistically sound and theoretically possible.

## Dataset Context
This dataset is a preprocessed version of the Portuguese bank marketing dataset, originally containing information about direct marketing campaigns (phone calls) conducted by a Portuguese banking institution from May 2008 to November 2010. The outcome variable of interest is 'y' (term deposit subscription).
The data reflects the state of affairs in **Portugal** around **May 2008 to November 2010**.

## Prediction Task
The goal is to build a model that predicts **whether the client subscribed to a term deposit (binary: yes/no)** (the `y` variable).

## Variables
- age: Age of the client, converted to categorical encoding (0-based indexing of age values). (datatype: Categorical (Ordinal/Integer))
- job: Type of job, preprocessed into 3 categories: 0=blue-collar, 1=management-service (admin/management/services), 2=other (all remaining job types). (datatype: Categorical (Nominal/Integer))
- marital: Marital status, , preprocessed into binary: 0=married, 1=non-married (divorced, single, or unknown). converted to categorical encoding (0-based indexing: divorced, married, single, unknown). (datatype: Categorical (Binary/Integer))
- education: Education level, converted to categorical encoding (0-based indexing of education levels). (datatype: Categorical (Ordinal/Integer))
- default: Has credit in default, binary encoded: 0=no, 1=yes. (datatype: Categorical (Binary/Integer))
- balance: Average yearly balance, preprocessed into binary: 0=zero balance, 1=positive balance. (datatype: Categorical (Binary/Integer))
- housing: Has housing loan, binary encoded: 0=no, 1=yes. (datatype: Categorical (Binary/Integer))
- loan: Has personal loan, binary encoded: 0=no, 1=yes. (datatype: Categorical (Binary/Integer))
- contact: Contact communication type, converted to categorical encoding (0-based indexing: cellular, telephone). (datatype: Categorical (Nominal/Integer))
- day: Last contact day of the month, preprocessed into binary: 0=day≤15, 1=day>15. (datatype: Categorical (Binary/Integer))
- month: Last contact month of year, converted to categorical encoding (0-based indexing of months). (datatype: Categorical (Nominal/Integer))
- duration: Last contact duration in seconds, preprocessed into 3 categories: 0=≤120 seconds, 1=121-600 seconds, 2=>600 seconds. Note: This attribute should be used with caution for realistic prediction as duration is not known before a call. (datatype: Categorical (Ordinal/Integer))
- campaign: Number of contacts performed during this campaign, preprocessed into 3 categories: 0=≤1 contact, 1=2-5 contacts, 2=>5 contacts. (datatype: Categorical (Ordinal/Integer))
- pdays: Number of days since last contact from previous campaign, preprocessed into 3 categories: 0=≤30 days, 1=31-180 days, 2=>180 days or never contacted (-1 in original data). (datatype: Categorical (Ordinal/Integer))
- previous: Number of contacts performed before this campaign, preprocessed into 3 categories: 0=no previous contacts, 1=1-5 previous contacts, 2=>5 previous contacts. (datatype: Categorical (Ordinal/Integer))
- poutcome: Outcome of the previous marketing campaign, converted to categorical encoding (0-based indexing: failure, nonexistent, success). (datatype: Categorical (Nominal/Integer))
- y: **TARGET OUTCOME** - Has the client subscribed a term deposit, binary encoded: 0=no, 1=yes. This is the target outcome variable for the analysis. (datatype: Categorical (Binary/Integer))

## Task: Propose Causal Edge Constraints

Provide two types of causal edge constraints based purely on statistical and theoretical considerations.

### 1. WHITELISTED EDGES (Must Exist - Deterministic Causal Links)
List only those directed edges (A → B) for which there is a **deterministic, universally-agreed causal relationship** based on physical laws, institutional rules, or mathematical definitions.

Format as:
- Source → Target: Brief justification referencing the deterministic rule or mechanism.

### 2. BLACKLISTED EDGES (Forbidden - Theoretical Impossibility)

**Type A - Theoretically Impossible:**
- List all directed edges where the **target is a root node**. For this dataset, the root nodes are **`age`**. These are fundamental demographic characteristics that are largely immutable or established early in life and influence subsequent financial behavior and opportunities. No other variable can cause them. **This includes edges between root nodes themselves** - root nodes cannot cause each other (e.g., if both `race_cat` and `male_cat` are root nodes, blacklist both (`race_cat`, `male_cat`) and (`male_cat`, `race_cat`) in both directions).
- Additionally, list any edges that violate temporal ordering or are logically impossible.

Format as:
- Source → Target: Explanation of why this is theoretically impossible (e.g., "Target is a root node", "Violates temporal order").

**IMPORTANT:** Do NOT include any edges in Type A that are blocked for fairness reasons. Only include edges that are statistically or theoretically impossible.
